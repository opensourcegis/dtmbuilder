Small closed contour filter

Closed loops enclosing less than 50 m² are omitted from display and export. Open lines remain. Set 0 to disable. A recognized projected metre/foot CRS is required for square-metre filtering.

Contour surface preparation

1 keeps the source grid. For a 0.1 m DTM, 5×5 block averaging produces a 0.5 m contour grid. Incomplete edge blocks are cropped; blocks touching NoData stay NoData.

Regional generalization

Three broad mean passes on the contour grid. 0 disables regional smoothing. This changes the contour surface, not the DTM.

Suppresses short contours in display and line export; it does not remove terrain errors. 0 keeps all lines.

Rectangular roof-like contours need DTM cleanup first. Regional smoothing cannot establish the true ground under objects and may suppress real banks or small landforms.
